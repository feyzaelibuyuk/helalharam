# World city data attribution

Source: GeoNames cities15000 export, https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip . Documentation: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt .

Copyright GeoNames contributors. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

Modified for Helal Rehber: converted tab-separated data into JSON; retained GeoNames id, name, country code, coordinates and alternative names only. Bundled snapshot contains 34,188 entries. Not a complete list of all settlements; names, borders and coordinates may be outdated or inaccurate. Snapshot is not updated automatically.

OSM restaurant data is fetched separately at user request and is not merged into this GeoNames dataset. OSM attribution/license: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (ODbL).
